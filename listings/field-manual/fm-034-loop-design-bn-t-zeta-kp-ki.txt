1. Inject a known, fixed offset - phase for Costas, timing for Gardner - comfortably inside the
   detector's linear region, not near the edges where it saturates or wraps.
2. Run it through costas_phase_error() or gardner_error() and log the raw output, averaged over
   enough symbols at that offset to wash out noise.
3. Repeat at a few more known offsets, both directions.
4. Kd is the slope of (detector output) vs. (known offset) across those points - a linear fit,
   not a two-point line, since real detectors bend near the edges of that region.
